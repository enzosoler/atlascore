# ADDITIONAL SPRINT SCOPE: LANDING PAGE + WAITLIST / APPLICATION FORM

atlas.core also needs a **high-converting landing page** and **one simple capture form**.

The goal is not to make a bloated marketing site.
The goal is to create the fastest possible path to:

* communicate the product clearly
* collect interested users
* validate demand
* drive installs / beta interest / paid interest
* support near-term monetization

This landing page must feel consistent with atlas.core:

* premium
* minimal
* polished
* Apple-like
* high trust
* not noisy
* not generic SaaS garbage

---

# LANDING PAGE OBJECTIVE

The landing page has one job:

> make a visitor immediately understand:

* what atlas.core is
* why it is different
* why it is useful now
* what they should do next

Primary message:

**Stop guessing. Run your body like a system.**

Supporting positioning:

atlas.core combines:

* nutrition
* training
* recovery
* hormones / protocols
* progress
* daily decision support

into one self-optimization system that tells you what to do next.

---

# LANDING PAGE STRATEGY

Do **not** build a giant multi-page marketing site right now.

Build **one strong landing page** with:

* clear hero
* product proof
* why it wins
* screenshot or feature proof
* CTA
* simple form

This is a conversion page, not a branding museum.

---

# LANDING PAGE REQUIRED SECTIONS

## 1. Hero section

Must immediately communicate:

### Headline

Use a strong direct headline such as:

* Stop guessing. Run your body like a system.
* The self-optimization OS for body composition, performance, and recovery.
* Know exactly what to do next with nutrition, training, recovery, and protocols.

### Subheadline

Explain the product clearly:
atlas.core helps you track your body, understand your current state, and get exact next actions across food, training, recovery, and protocols.

### Primary CTA

Examples:

* Join the beta
* Get early access
* Start optimizing
* Request access

### Secondary CTA

Examples:

* See how it works
* View product preview

### Hero visual

Prefer:

* one premium product screenshot
* or a tight composition of 2-3 screens max
* ideally Today screen centered because that is the core wedge

Do not clutter the hero with too many screenshots.

---

## 2. Core value section

Explain the main loop:

**Detect -> Interpret -> Decide -> Execute -> Adapt**

Translate that into user language:

* See where you are now
* Know if you're on track
* Get your next best action
* Log fast
* Let the system adapt with you

This section should make atlas.core feel different from ordinary tracking apps.

---

## 3. Why atlas.core is different

Use 3-5 concise value pillars.

Recommended pillars:

### Simple on the surface

Fast logging, clear daily view, low friction

### Intelligent underneath

Predictions, recommendations, interpretation, cause-effect

### Built for execution

Not just data -- exact next actions

### Adaptive system

Plans and recommendations evolve with your progress

### One system, not five apps

Nutrition, training, recovery, progress, and protocols in one loop

Do not write generic feature soup.

---

## 4. Product proof section

Show screenshots or cards proving actual utility.

Recommended examples:

* Today screen with on-track / off-track state
* Next best action card
* Nutrition logging / meal analysis
* Training or progress screen
* Protocol / recovery / trend proof if clean

Each proof card should have:

* short title
* one-line benefit
* screenshot or UI crop

---

## 5. Outcome / transformation section

This should answer:
"What do I get if I use this consistently?"

Examples:

* Better adherence
* Less guesswork
* More consistent body composition progress
* Better daily decision-making
* Clearer control over training, nutrition, recovery, and protocols

Keep it concrete.

---

## 6. Social proof / trust section

If real proof exists, show it.

Possible proof:

* real user quotes
* usage counts
* tester quotes
* founder story with credibility
* product screenshots that look real and in-use

If hard proof is limited, use trust-building structure instead of fake claims.

Do not invent numbers.

---

## 7. CTA section

End with a clean CTA block.

Example:
**Start using a system that tells you what to do next.**
Join the beta / request access / get launch updates.

---

# FORM STRATEGY

You need **one simple form**.

Do not make it long.
The form's job is to capture high-intent leads fast.

Use either:

* embedded form on the page
* or CTA opens modal / Typeform / Tally / native form

But keep it visually consistent.

---

# FORM OBJECTIVE

Capture:

* potential users
* beta testers
* early buyers
* people interested in launch

The form should help qualify demand without creating friction.

---

# FORM REQUIRED FIELDS

Keep it short.

## Minimum version

* Name
* Email
* Primary goal

## Better version

* Name
* Email
* Primary goal
* What are you trying to improve most right now?
* What do you currently use to track or manage this?
* Would you be interested in beta access, early paid access, or both?

---

# FORM FIELD RECOMMENDATIONS

### Name

Simple text field

### Email

Required

### Primary goal

Dropdown or chips:

* fat loss
* muscle gain
* performance
* recovery
* hormone / protocol tracking
* overall optimization

### What are you trying to improve most right now?

Short answer

### What do you currently use?

Short answer or multiple choice:

* MyFitnessPal
* workout app
* notes
* spreadsheet
* multiple apps
* nothing consistent

### Interest type

* beta access
* early paid access
* both

This helps qualify monetization immediately.

---

# FORM UX RULES

* keep it short
* mobile-first
* no unnecessary friction
* make submit feel immediate
* show a polished success state

Success message example:
"You're in. We'll reach out as atlas.core opens early access."

Optional:

* ask if they want to follow launch updates
* send to waitlist confirmation page
* offer TestFlight invite later
