atlas.core · iOS Design Pack

Health, training, nutrition, and professional workflows in one native-feeling mobile system.

This directory is the unified handoff layer for the atlas.core mobile design work: dark-mode-first, iOS-native navigation, large titles, muted teal accents, disciplined card surfaces, and dedicated galleries for athlete, coach, nutritionist, clinician, admin, and system flows.
Coverage
130 screens
Public onboarding, athlete product, pro roles, admin tools, paywall, help, and system edge cases.
Platform feel
Native iOS
SF Pro hierarchy, bottom tab bar, safe areas, large-title collapse, drag-handle sheets, focused full-screen flows.
Accent discipline
Sparse teal
Primary actions and active states only; all other UI keeps a calm neutral dark palette.

Design System Snapshot

Shared tokens and interaction rules used across every screen family in the galleries.

Deep Background
#080E0E
Primary app canvas and modal backdrops. Used to keep contrast premium and quiet.
Card Surface
#111A1A
Cards, rows, KPI tiles, list cells, and most contained surfaces. Default radius: 14px.
Separator Border
#1E2C2C
Low-contrast dividers, card outlines, tab bar edges, and field boundaries.
Accent Teal
#4A9B9B
Reserved for primary actions, selected states, metric emphasis, and focused controls.
Destructive
#E05C5C
Delete account, reset progress, expired or abnormal states, and critical alerts.
Success
#4CAF7D
Completed sets, normal markers, healthy adherence, and confirmation states.
14px cards 10px buttons 20px sheets 16px horizontal padding 8px spacing rhythm Haptic-friendly tap targets

Screen Matrix

The requested product sections are preserved as separate conceptual families even when galleries are grouped by screen range.

Athlete product

Daily guidance and execution

Today, nutrition, workouts, body tracking, insights, diary, and Atlas AI form the athlete command center.
Professional roles

Coach, nutritionist, clinician

Operational dashboards prioritize adherence, exceptions, invitation flows, and credible prescribing experiences.
Commercial layer

Trials, pricing, upgrade gates

Conversion surfaces stay premium and restrained, with persuasive hierarchy but no cheap SaaS treatment.
System integrity

Help, loading, and edge cases

Offline, expired sessions, 404s, role detection, invite linking, and guides are designed intentionally rather than as afterthoughts.
