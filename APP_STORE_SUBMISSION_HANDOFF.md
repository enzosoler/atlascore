## Step 2 — Upload from Organizer

1. Click **Distribute App** in Organizer.
2. Choose **App Store Connect** → **Upload**.
3. Distribution options:
   - "Include bitcode" → leave at default (Apple deprecated this; usually off).
   - "Strip Swift symbols" → ON.
   - "Upload your app's symbols" → ON (helps Sentry crash symbolication too).
   - "Manage Version and Build Number" → I recommend **OFF** so you control what gets uploaded.
4. Distribution signing: **Automatically manage signing** is the safe default unless you have a reason to use manual.
5. Click **Upload**. Wait ~5–15 min depending on bundle.
6. **Stop and report back if you get any of these:**
   - "Invalid Code Signing Entitlements"
   - "Missing Push Notification Entitlement"
   - "App Store Connect Operation Error"
   - "ITMS-90683" or any `ITMS-` error → these are App Store Connect rejections at upload time.

After upload, wait 5–30 min for Apple to process. The build will appear under the
**TestFlight** tab in App Store Connect with status "Processing", then become
selectable as a build for the App Store version.

- Crop nothing. App Store wants the full simulator frame minus the simulator chrome (use the simulator's "Save Screen" not a screen-region capture).

**Do not include:**
