charts that tell
the body's story.

Six chart primitives, every dashboard in atlas.core is built from. Each primitive is tuned to one thing and does only that. No dual-axis, no pie, no 3D. The rule: a chart earns its place by changing a decision — if it doesn't change what the user does next, it's furniture.

/// rules & anti-patterns

the short list.

  • One chart, one question. If a chart answers two questions, split it.
  • Accent is a noun. Yellow = "this is the point." Never decorative.
  • Ink = truth. Dim = context. Never color-code categories with the accent.
  • Axes are a last resort. Annotate the thing that matters inline; ditch the grid.
  • No tooltips on phones. If the data needs hover, it doesn't belong on mobile.
  • No pies. No donuts. Bar stacks express ratios more honestly.
  • Time flows right. Always. "Now" is always on the right edge.
  • Reduced motion kills the draw-in; data appears instant.
