/// atlas.core · ios · widgets · iOS 17+

glanceable, not
a second app.

Five widgets across small, medium, large, and lock screen — designed against Apple's HIG. One flagship Today widget in three sizes carries the daily dashboard onto the home screen. The rest are focused: next workout, fuel rings, quick log, and a quiet weekly load summary. No streaks. No leaderboards. No noise. Type ramp follows SF Pro Display / Rounded; corner radius is the system 22pt; padding is Apple's default 16pt.

in context.

Home screen (light + dark) and lock screen, sized to iPhone 15 Pro. Widgets sit on real wallpaper, not a flat bg, so blur and contrast are honest.

today.

The flagship. Mirrors the daily dashboard. Ships in all three home sizes; the large is the one Apple Featured editors would show.

today

w1 · small / medium / large

purpose

One glance answers "what does today ask of me?" Workout queued, fuel progress, checklist of the day's commitments.

data shown

  • Today's planned workout (name + exercise count + duration)
  • Calories logged / target with macro ring
  • Macros: protein, carbs, fat (large only)
  • Daily checklist with completion state (large only)

tap behavior

  • Small: deep-link atlascore://today
  • Medium left half: atlascore://workout/today · right half: atlascore://nutrition
  • Large: per-row deep links — workout row, fuel row, each checklist item

states

  • Empty: "No workout planned" · CTA "Pick a routine"
  • Rest day: dim glyph + "Rest day · recover"
  • Loading: skeleton bars at 30% opacity, no spinner (HIG)
  • Error / no auth: atlas.core wordmark + "Open app to refresh"

next workout

w2 · small · accent variant

purpose

For users who only want one widget on the home screen. The accent fill makes it the "do this next" focal point — mirrors the brand's bolt mark.

data shown

  • Day + scheduled time
  • Workout name (Push A, Pull B, etc.)
  • Program week / day position

tap behavior

Deep-link atlascore://workout/next. App launches into the prepared session — first set primed.

states

  • Mid-workout: swaps to "In progress · 2 of 5"
  • Rest day: "Rest · next: Pull B Wed"
  • No program: "Browse routines →"

macros · fuel

w3 · small

purpose

The single most-glanced number for nutrition users — calories logged vs target, with macros at the bottom. Ring fills as the day fills.

refresh

Updates on every meal log via WidgetCenter reloadTimelines. Background refresh budget: every 15 min when idle.

tap behavior

Deep-link atlascore://nutrition/today

states

  • Empty: ring at 0%, "Log your first meal"
  • Over target: ring fills past 100% in muted accent (no red — not punishing)
  • No target set: shows raw kcal only, no ring

quick log

w4 · medium · interactive (iOS 17)

purpose

iOS 17 brought interactive widgets. This is the one place we use them — three taps to log a meal, cardio session, or weigh-in without launching the app.

buttons

  • Meal: opens a quick-add sheet via AppIntent (1 tap from home screen)
  • Cardio: starts a cardio timer in background
  • Weigh: presents number-pad sheet for body weight
  • More: launches the app at quick-log root

fallback

iOS 16 and earlier: buttons render as a non-interactive grid; tapping anywhere deep-links into the app's quick-log screen. No degraded feel.

this week

w5 · medium · no streaks

purpose

Honest weekly load. Seven dots: completed / today / planned / rest. No flame icons, no streaks, no shame. The brand promises "calm by design" — this widget proves it.

data shown

  • Week N of program
  • Completion ratio (e.g., 2 of 4 done)
  • Per-day status pip (Mon → Sun, week starts on user's preferred first day)

tap behavior

Whole widget → atlascore://program/week. Each pip is a tap target on iOS 17 → that day's session.

lock screen.

All three Apple lock-screen complication shapes. Designed for materially translucent rendering — white-on-glass, no fills.

lock complications

circular · rectangular · inline

circular · 72pt

Two configurations: FUEL (calories % to target) and WEEK (workouts done / scheduled). Ring + label + value. Renders monochrome white per HIG.

rectangular · 158×72pt

Next workout. Time, name, exercise count. Single tap unlocks → workout screen.

inline · single line

Above the clock. Today's whole status in one sentence: workout · fuel · week. Clamps to ~50 chars; truncates with ellipsis.

smart suggestions.

Which widgets the system should recommend, and when.

first launch
After onboarding, donate WidgetKit intent for Today (medium) — Apple's Smart Stack picks it up. We don't nag with a permission sheet.
7am – 10am local
Surface Next Workout in Smart Stack (rotation hint: .relevance .high) on workout days; Macros on rest days.
11:30am – 1:30pm
Surface Quick Log medium. Lunch is the most-skipped meal log — proximity matters.
workout in 30 min
Next Workout small bumps to top of Smart Stack with .relevance .critical. Lock screen rectangular goes live too.
end of week
This Week surfaces Sunday evening. Quiet recap, not a push.
personalization
User can configure Today widget via long-press → "Edit Widget" → toggle show fuel / show checklist / compact. Settings persist via App Group.

motion + refresh.

Widgets don't animate. They update. Here's the cadence.

timeline cadence
Standard: 15 min between reloads (system budget). Today + Macros additionally trigger WidgetCenter.reloadTimelines on every meal/set log.
subtle animation
iOS 17 .contentTransition(.numericText) on kcal value and ring fill — that's it. No bounces. No celebrations. (Celebrations live in-app via the ECG curve; widgets stay still.)
interactive feedback
Quick Log buttons: standard system tap (haptic + 80ms scale-down). After AppIntent fires: ring redraws, last-logged caption updates. No success toast — the change is the feedback.
reduce motion
All numeric transitions become instant. Ring still fills but without the 320ms ease.

/// rules & anti-patterns

the short list.

  • One number per widget. The hero glyph is whatever single thing the user came to glance at.
  • Accent is a finger, not a wash. Sulfur fills only on Next Workout (small) and ring fills.
  • No streaks. No flames. No "X days strong." Brand discipline.
  • Type ramp is SF Pro Display + SF Pro Rounded only. Archivo Black stays in-app.
  • Corner radius is system-given. Don't override. Apple's mask matches the wallpaper bevel.
  • Lock screen is monochrome. Tints arrive from the wallpaper; we don't fight it.
  • Tap targets ≥ 44pt. Quick Log buttons measure 50×50pt actual.
  • Empty states get a verb. "Pick a routine," "Log your first meal." Never just "No data."
