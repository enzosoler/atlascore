/// app store connect · notes

before you paste.

  • Title + Subtitle are both indexed by Apple search — front-load search terms in keywords rather than repeat them in the title.
  • Keywords field is comma-separated, no spaces (Apple ignores spaces but counts them). Both sit under 100 chars.
  • Don't repeat words between Title, Subtitle, and Keywords — Apple indexes all three together.
  • "Coach" / "coaching" only appears in keywords for A — swap into B if coaching becomes a paid hook.
  • Neither version makes medical, diagnostic, or outcome claims. Safe for App Review on Health & Fitness category.
  • Promotional Text (170 char) is editable without resubmitting a build — use it for limited-time messaging.
  • Bullets are for the marketing site / press kit. App Store description has no native bullet UI; the field renders as a paragraph block.
  • If localizing, translate Subtitle and Keywords first — they move the needle more than the long description.
